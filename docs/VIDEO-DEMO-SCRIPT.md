## 1. Product Narrative Arc: From Fragmented Notes to an AI Digital Twin

Our daily lives and thoughts are deeply fractured:
- Work breakthroughs and architecture decisions get lost in Slack channels and Jira threads.
- Deep reading insights and book notes get buried in Notion or desktop text files.
- Fleeting moments of personal reflection vanish into mobile memos and camera rolls.

When we try to look back, our authentic self is nowhere to be found.

**Dayprint solves this at the root.** It is a tactile, local-first memory sanctuary that gathers every fragment of your life—engineering breakthroughs, daily habits, reading notes, and foundational principles—and weaves them into an evolving neural constellation. Dayprint is not just another note-taking app; it is the **cradle of your AI Digital Twin and Cognitive Confidant **.

### 🎬 Scene 2: Today's Canvas & Creation Ritual (0:25 – 0:55 | 30s)

* **Key Message:** Zero-friction capture, instant AI Polish into STAR brag format, temporal Weave Echo, custom type creation, and Morandi paper pigments.
* **On-Screen Visual Actions:**
  1. **Focus Input:** Click the breathing input surface at the top of the canvas (`TodayEditor`). The writing desk smoothly expands.
  2. **Type Raw Note:**
     - Title Input: `"Async Actor Isolation Engine"`
     - Content Textarea: `"Implemented memory state locks for high-throughput node synchronization under distributed workloads."`
  3. **AI Polish in Action:**
     - Click the glowing **"AI Polish"** button (`[title*="Polish"]`).
     - A subtle shimmer animates across the input as the AI distills the raw thought.
     - The golden **STAR Container** unfolds below the textarea, cleanly structuring:
       - **S · Situation & Context:** *"Distributed memory contention causing high latency spikes under multi-agent loads."*
       - **A · Action Taken:** *"Architected asynchronous actor isolation locks with lock-free atomic buffers."*
       - **R · Impact & Results:** *"Reduced sync contention by 64% and achieved sub-millisecond p99 state propagation."*
  4. **Weave Echo:**
     - Highlight the amber **Memory Echo Bar**: `"Origin: Technology Selection Framework"`.
     - Click the **"Weave Echo"** button (`[title*="Weave"]`).
     - The button transforms into an emerald badge: `"Weaved Link"`, weaving an organic temporal thread between today's implementation and a past architectural decision.
  5. **Type Selection & Custom Type Creation:**
     - Show the category row (`Idea`, `Learning`, `Moment`, `Work`, `Review`).
     - Click the **"+ New Type"** button. An inline input appears.
     - Type `"Architecture"` and confirm. The new custom type badge is instantly created and selected!
  6. **Morandi Color Pigments & Topics:**
     - Click the **Type Color Palette** icon (`[title*="Change synced color"]`). A Morandi pigment grid appears (Terracotta, Amber, Sage, Fjord, Twilight, etc.).
     - Pick **Fjord Blue (`#5B7B94`)** — a tooltip confirms: *"Updates all cards of this type globally"*.
     - Type `#distributed-systems, #actor-model` into the topics input.
  7. **Print / Save Action:**
     - Press `⌘ + Enter` (or click `"Save / Print"`).
     - Confetti explodes, a crisp mechanical typewriter key snaps, and a serrated thermal receipt tears off and drops onto the canvas!
* **SFX:** Mechanical typewriter keystrokes (`Click-Clack`), AI chime, and thermal paper tear-off (`Zzz-Rip!`).
* **English Voiceover Script:**
  > *"Capturing a moment is completely frictionless. No title paralysis, no rigid folder trees.  
  > Write down a raw thought, and click **AI Polish**. Dayprint’s embedded intelligence immediately structures your rough notes into a quantifiable STAR accomplishment — Situation, Task, Action, and Result — ready for your career brag sheet.  
  > Notice the **Memory Echo** bar: Dayprint detects past thoughts that resonate with what you just wrote. With one click on **Weave Echo**, you weave an authentic temporal thread connecting today's code with your past architectural foundations.  
  > Categorize with flexible types, create custom types like 'Architecture' on the fly, bind signature Morandi paper pigments, and tag your topics.  
  > Press `Command+Enter`, hear the mechanical keystroke, and your thought prints as a physical slip onto your canvas."*

### 🎬 Scene 5: Knowledge Graph — Force Graph, Cards Mode & Link Management (1:40 – 2:05 | 25s)

* **Key Message:** Relational thinking via Force Graph physics, 5-tier cognitive Cards mode, and dynamic link management (disconnecting and connecting with causal rationale).
* **On-Screen Visual Actions:**
  1. Click **"Knowledge Graph"** in the navigation sidebar.
  2. **Dynamic Force Graph Mode:**
     - The 2D canvas springs to life with organic physics simulation.
     - Drag a node; watch connected constellation threads flex and rebound with spring tension.
     - Hover over `Async Actor Isolation Engine` — connected nodes (`Product Architecture Focus`, `Technology Selection Framework`) glow with soft indigo energy while surrounding nodes gently dim.
  3. **Switch to Cards Mode:**
     - Click the **"Cards"** segmented control button (`button:has-text("Cards")`).
     - The screen smoothly transitions from the force graph into the structured **Cognitive Cards Matrix**.
     - Point out the 5 cognitive tiers:
       - `L0 · Raw Moments`
       - `L1 · Structured Events`
       - `L2 · Habits & SOPs`
       - `L3 · Mental Models`
       - `L4 · Core Beliefs & First Principles`
  4. **Live Link Management (Disconnect & Connect):**
     - Switch back to the Graph view. Click on `Async Actor Isolation Engine` to open the side inspector.
     - Click the **"Link Node"** button (`button:has-text("Link Node")`).
     - The **Connection Management Modal (`ConnectionModal`)** appears:
       - **Disconnect Example:** Under *Existing Connections*, locate an outdated link. Click the **"Disconnect"** button. The link instantly dissolves with a smooth fade.
       - **Connect Example:** In the *Add New Connection* section:
         - Select Target Memory: `Product Architecture Focus`.
         - Type Causal Rationale: `"Direct architectural predecessor and state isolation foundation"`.
         - Click **"Connect"**.
       - Confetti pops, the modal closes, and a glowing new relational edge beams directly between the two nodes in the constellation!
* **SFX:** Soft gravitational hum, laser chime on link connect, confetti burst.
* **English Voiceover Script:**
  > *"Memories never exist in isolation. In the **Knowledge Graph**, Dayprint weaves your scattered reflections into a living neural constellation.

  > Switch seamlessly to **Cards Mode** to inspect your thoughts categorized across a 5-tier cognitive hierarchy — from fleeting moments to habits, mental models, and core principles.  
  > And managing links is completely transparent. Open Link Management to prune outdated connections with one click, or establish new causal bridges — articulating why two ideas connect.  
  > Your knowledge network is no longer a static graveyard; it is an active, self-organizing mind."*
