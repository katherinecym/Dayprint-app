## 1. Storage & Ownership

Dayprint is built on a strict privacy and data sovereignty foundation:
- **Zero Central Database**: Your personal notes, emotional logs, and career milestones never transit through proprietary third-party servers.
- **Immediate Browser Persistence**: Everything is safely persisted in your browser instantly upon typing.
- **100% User Custody**: Your memories belong exclusively to you, exportable as plain Markdown files and standard JSON snapshots at any time.

---

## 2. Setup & Demo

- **Getting Started**: Run `pnpm dev` and visit `http://localhost:3000`.
- **Demo Mode vs. Production Mode**:
  - *Demo Mode*: Pre-populates sample thermal receipts, cognitive matrices, and knowledge graph links for immediate evaluation.
  - *Production Mode*: Offers a clean slate for authentic daily journaling with an instant 1-click restore mechanism.

---

## 3. Tab 1: Daily Canvas & Today's Notes

- **Frictionless Capture**: Focus the bottom dock (`Cmd+K`), type your thought, select an optional Morandi tag (Insight, Mood, Work, Life), and hit `Cmd+Enter`.
- **Tactile Feedback**: Experience realistic receipt tear animations paired with programmatic mechanical typing audio.
- **Responsive Masonry Feed**: Cards arrange organically by chronological date headers with washi-tape aesthetics and keyword search.

---

## 4. Tab 2: Cognitive Ledger

- **5-Tier Cognitive Hierarchy (L0–L4)**:
  - `L0 Raw Stream`: Ephemeral daily fragments and quick observations.
  - `L1 Daily Clusters`: Structured experiences and chronological logs.
  - `L2 Habits & SOPs`: Reusable routines, behavioral guidelines, and practices.
  - `L3 Mental Models`: Conceptual frameworks and strategic perspectives.
  - `L4 Core Values`: Guiding life principles and personal north stars.
- **STAR Achievement Transformer**: Turn rough work reflections into quantifiable Situation-Task-Action-Result bullet points for resumes and reviews.

---

## 5. Tab 3: Knowledge Graph

- **Interactive Constellation Canvas**: Visualizes organic connections between thoughts across career and life.
- **Bidirectional Links**: Use `[[wiki-links]]` to weave memories into an interconnected cognitive neural network.
- **Resonance Illumination**: Hovering over any thought illuminates related memories and cross-domain themes.

---

## 6. Daily Thermal Receipts & Mood Washes

- **Authentic Paper Texture**: Serrated tear edges, tactile paper grain, and Morandi mood wash backgrounds.
- **Daily Compression**: Condenses all logs from a single calendar day into an elegant, collectible physical receipt snapshot.

---

## 7. Weekly Digest & Cognitive Distillation

- **Natural-Week Review**: Synthesizes the week's slips into Key Insights, Emotional Arcs, and Completed Milestones.
- **Full CRUD Controls**: Edit, add, or prune memory items directly within the modal.
- **Markdown Export**: Download clean, standardized `week-YYYY-WNN.md` reports with one click.

---

## 8. GitHub Silent Archival

- **Personal Access Token (PAT)**: Configure your private repository to enable background synchronization.
- **Debounced Commits**: Automatically packages and pushes clean Markdown files and JSON snapshots every few seconds.
- **Keepalive Protection**: In-flight sync requests complete safely even on abrupt browser tab exit.
