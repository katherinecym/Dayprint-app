Section 1 · Foundation

100% Data Sovereignty & AI Digital Twin Sanctuary

Modern human thoughts are deeply fractured across Slack, Jira, Notion, and phone memos. Dayprint is a tactile, local-first sanctuary that compiles every moment of your life directly into your private GitHub repository as clean Markdown.

Principle 1

Zero Data Leakage

No central databases, zero telemetry tracking. All personal memories, daily receipts, and relationship edges are committed straight to your private GitHub repository.

  • Client-side GitHub API integration
  • Token stored only in local browser
  • Standard human-readable Markdown
Principle 2

Tactile Sensory Craft

Stepping away from cold glass interfaces. Dayprint embraces warm Morandi paper fibers, mechanical typewriter audio feedback, and physical jagged-edge receipt slips.

  • Subtle noise maps and paper texture
  • Dip-dye mood wash watercolor glow
  • Physical thermal receipt sawtooth cuts
Principle 3

Cognitive Evolution

Moving beyond raw note dumps. Dayprint organizes knowledge into a vertical L0~L4 cognitive pyramid paired with a horizontal semantic wikilink topology.

  • L0 Raw Notes up to L4 Core Beliefs
  • Source provenance with source_ids
  • Obsidian-compatible YAML frontmatter
