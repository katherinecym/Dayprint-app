Overview · Storage & Ideas

Your Notes Stay in Your Hands

Most of what we build and learn ends up scattered across Slack discussions, temporary text files, and phone memos. When you actually need them later—for a promotion review, an engineering retrospective, or recalling how you solved a tough bug three months ago—they are almost impossible to find. Dayprint gives you a clean space to capture daily thoughts and syncs them straight to your private GitHub repository as plain Markdown files.

Storage

Direct GitHub Sync

No company databases and no proprietary file formats. Every note, daily receipt, and link is saved as standard Markdown in your private repository.

  • Connects straight to GitHub without middleman servers
  • Your token stays encrypted in your own browser only
  • Open and edit your files anytime in Obsidian or VS Code
Look & Feel

Warm Paper Style

Instead of a harsh white screen, Dayprint uses gentle paper colors, thermal receipt edges, and soft typing sounds that feel comfortable for daily writing.

  • Warm paper background that is easy on the eyes
  • 5 watercolor mood washes for your daily recap
  • Torn-paper edges on daily summary receipts
Organization

Structured Notes

Not all notes carry the same weight. Dayprint helps you organize quick scratchpads into reusable habits, mental models, and principles you work by.

  • Sort notes from quick scratchpads up to key rules
  • Clickable links back to where each idea first started
  • Automatic suggestions for notes discussing related topics

Fast Writing

Ready-to-Type Editor & AI Polish

  • Separate Chinese and English tabs that do not overwrite each other
  • Weave Echo brings up past notes from 2-4 weeks ago on related topics

The right-hand panel gives you today's timeline, an evening reflection field that syncs with today's receipt, an annual activity heatmap, and an instant 5-color mood picker.

Refining Ideas · Cognitive Ledger

Tab 2: Cognitive Ledger (Memory OS)

The place where you refine and organize your thinking. Instead of keeping all notes in one flat list, you can group them into five clear stages, see your confidence in each rule, and click through to where each idea originated.

Click to enlarge
Five Levels

From Raw Scratchpads to Principles

Sorts notes from L0 quick thoughts to L1 key wins, L2 workflows, L3 thinking models, and L4 core principles. You can also use AI Distill to turn recent notes into candidate principles.

  • Ranked so cards needing edits or translations float to the top
  • Visible confidence score bar (85% and up green, 70% amber, under 70% red)
  • Clean bilingual readiness tags: ZH Ready · EN Pending
Traceability

Source Links Back to Original Notes

Higher-level rules display 'Derived from N notes'. Click the link to jump straight back to the original daily entry where the idea first started, so every rule is grounded in real experience.

  • Click to inspect original notes behind each principle
  • Edit text and topic tags directly on the card without modal dialogs
  • Exports standard frontmatter compatible with Obsidian
Connecting Notes · Knowledge Graph

Tab 3: Knowledge Graph

Notes become much more useful when they link to each other. Explore connections visually through the interactive graph canvas, or switch to the Topology Index to find your most connected hub topics.

Visual Map

Interactive Graph & Auto-Link

Related notes cluster together on the screen. Clicking 'Auto-Link Graph' scans your notes and suggests connections between cards discussing similar topics.

  • Smooth zoom, pan, and drag to explore connections
  • Auto-Link suggests connections you might have missed
  • Nodes are color-coded by their note stage
Clean Overview

Topology Index (Most Connected Topics)

If you just want a clean list of which topics are mentioned the most, switch to the Topology Index. It lists your key topics by how many notes connect to them, without cluttering the screen with full paragraphs.

  • Ranked with the most connected notes at the top
  • Clean compact view: type + level + title + connection count
  • Clicking any topic highlights it in the visual map
